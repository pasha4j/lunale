How to play Españle

A Spanish-speaking country or territory lights up on the map.

  • Round 1: type the country's name.
  • Round 2: type its capital (the red dot), with 5 new hearts.
  • Every key is a try. A letter that can't continue the right name costs a ♥. Correct letters stick.
  • Suggestions show up after 3 letters (or never, in hard mode ⚙). Tapping a wrong one also costs a ♥.
  • Spanish or English both count (España or Spain), and you don't need accents.
  • Hearts left are your points: up to 10 per country, 21 countries per run.
